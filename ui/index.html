HITL checklist

      

      

        How HITL works here. The Pixhawk runs its normal firmware. The simulator feeds it IMU, magnetometer,
        barometer and GPS over USB and takes its motor outputs back into the physics. Steps: plug the board in over
        USB, click Connect, then Enable HITL if the checklist asks for it (sets SYS_HITL = 1, saves and reboots),
        then Push geometry. QGroundControl keeps working through UDP 14550, but disable its
        serial auto-connect (Application Settings → General → AutoConnect) or it will grab the USB port first.
